For designers producing social media materials (Meta feed, Reels/Stories, carousels, Google Display). Every value here is taken from the live product, so anything you make will sit next to the app without a seam. Source of truth: src/index.css, tailwind.config.js, src/components/ui/.

1. The style in one line

Neo-brutalist, playful, loud. Fat black borders, hard shadows with zero blur, chunky uppercase type, saturated flat colour, and things tilted a few degrees off-square. Nothing soft, nothing gradient-y, nothing corporate.

It should feel like a sticker pack or a street poster, not an ed-tech dashboard. The competitors (Duolingo, Babbel) are rounded and friendly-corporate; we are deliberately harder-edged and more handmade. The one non-negotiable: every surface is outlined and every outlined thing casts a solid offset shadow.

2. Logo

The mark is a Barcelos rooster — the Portuguese folk symbol — reduced to flat geometry. It signals the product's Portuguese roots and its "real language, real culture" positioning. It is never redrawn, recoloured or outlined differently.

Lockup

Rooster sits on a white rounded plate (14px radius, 3px navy border, 5px yellow offset shadow), wordmark to the right in Outfit 900, uppercase, tight tracking. Gap between plate and wordmark = ¼ of the plate width.

Clear space & minimum size

  • Clear space on all sides = the height of the plate's corner radius × 2 (roughly 28px at 64px plate).
  • Minimum plate size: 40px on screen. Below that use the rooster alone, no wordmark.
  • Profile pictures / avatars: rooster on a white plate, full bleed. Never on yellow — the beak and legs are yellow and disappear.

✓ Do

  • Keep the rooster on white or a very light plate.
  • Scale it uniformly; let it rotate up to 12° as a playful element.
  • Use the rooster alone as a small stamp, watermark (20% opacity) or sticker.

✗ Don't

  • Recolour the body, comb or tail.
  • Add a drop shadow with blur, a gradient, or a glow.
  • Put the wordmark in any font other than Outfit 900 uppercase.
  • Place the rooster directly on yellow or on a busy photo.

Colour ratio for a social asset

Roughly 60% background (white / paper blue / navy) · 25% one hero colour (yellow or blue) · 15% ink (borders + type) · a pinch of rose. Two accent colours max per asset, plus navy. More than that and it stops reading as one brand.

Dark mode

The app has a full dark theme and campaign assets may use it for contrast in a feed. The one rule that changes: on dark, the offset shadow becomes yellow (#FACC15), not navy — a navy shadow is invisible on a navy page. Cards go #1E293B with #334155 borders and their own #1E293B shadow.

4. Typography

Outfit is the only typeface. Free on Google Fonts. Weights in use: 400 (body), 600 (emphasis), 800, 900 (everything loud).

fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit — if Outfit is unavailable, fall back to a geometric grotesque with a true Black: Poppins, Archivo or Montserrat Black. Never a serif, never a script.

Rules

  • Headlines: 900, UPPERCASE, tracking tight (−2% to −3%), line-height 0.9. They should feel packed.
  • Buttons & labels: 900, UPPERCASE, tracking wide (+8% to +14%) at small sizes.
  • Body: 400–600, sentence case, normal tracking, line-height 1.5.
  • Subtitles are italic 600 — this is a brand tic from the landing page, keep it.
  • Never lowercase a headline. Never letterspace body copy.

5. The neo-brutalist kit

Five mechanics. Get these right and anything you draw is on-brand.

① Borders

  • 4px solid navy is the default on every card, button and box.
  • 2–3px for small chips and the logo plate. 8px for a headline underline.
  • Borders are always solid, always navy on light; #334155 on dark.
  • Scale up on large canvases: on a 1080px square, use 8–10px so it reads the same.

② Hard shadows

  • Zero blur, zero spread. Offset down-right.
  • Standard offsets: 4px 4px (buttons), 6px 6px (cards), 10px 10px (hover / feature).
  • Colour: navy #0F172A on light, yellow #FACC15 on dark.
  • On a 1080px canvas scale to 10–16px.

③ Corner radii

  • Pills / CTAs: fully rounded (999px).
  • Cards: 16px (rounded-2xl) · Buttons: 12px · Feature cards: 32px.
  • Never square corners, never a mix of radii on one object.

④ Tilt

  • Rotate elements ±1°, ±2°, ±6° or ±12° — nothing in between, and never more than 12°.
  • Alternate direction between neighbours so a row of cards zig-zags.
  • Banners and marquee bands sit at −2° and bleed off both edges.
  • Hero highlight box: −2°.

⑤ Motion (for Reels / animated ads)

6. Imagery & graphic elements

Photography

  • Real, candid, unposed. People talking in markets, cafés, on trains, with family — the situations the product is for.
  • Warm, high-saturation, natural light. No corporate stock, no headsets, no people pointing at laptops.
  • Always framed: a photo goes inside a navy-bordered box with an offset shadow, often tilted. A bare photo is off-brand.
  • Optional: knock the subject out and place them over a flat yellow field.

Icons & decoration

  • Icon set is Lucide (lucide.dev) — outline, 2–3px stroke, matches the border language. Free and open source.
  • Decorative extras: speech bubbles with fat borders, hand-drawn arrows, stars/sparkles, flag chips, tilted stickers.
  • No gradients, glassmorphism, soft drop shadows, 3D renders, or AI-slop illustration.
  • Flags are fine for dialect callouts (🇵🇹 🇧🇷 🇪🇸 🇲🇽 🇬🇧 🇺🇸) — put them in bordered chips, not floating.

8. Voice in graphics

The single hardest rule: we never say "learn" or "learning". The word frames the product as homework. We say practice — you practice a language while doing something you already want to do.

Also avoid: course, curriculum, lessons (as our offer), streak, master a language, fluent in 3 months, AI-powered.

✓ Words we use

  • practice · keep it warm · sound local
  • real slang · expressions people actually use
  • alongside your teacher · in your language
  • 5 minutes · free to start

✗ Words we don't

  • learn / learning · course · curriculum
  • streak · master a language
  • fluent in 3 months · unlock your potential
  • AI-powered · revolutionary · seamless

Headline shape: short, second person, a little confrontational. "Day 600. Still can't order a coffee like a local?" · "You understand your family. Now answer them." · "Keep your tutor. Keep your app. Fill the gap between them." Caps, tight, no exclamation marks stacked.

9. Accessibility floor

  • Never white text on Brand Yellow (≈1.2:1 — invisible). Yellow always pairs with navy.
  • Minimum 4.5:1 for body copy, 3:1 for headline-size type. Navy on white/paper/yellow all pass comfortably.
  • Never carry meaning in colour alone — pair with a label or an icon.
  • Reels: burn in captions. Assume sound off.
  • Keep the tilt on type under 3° — beyond that it gets hard to read at feed size.

10. Quick checklist before you export

  • ☐ Is there yellow in the frame?
  • ☐ Does every box have a 4px+ navy border and a hard, zero-blur offset shadow?
  • ☐ Is at least one element tilted (±1/2/6/12°)?
  • ☐ Is the headline Outfit 900, uppercase, tight-tracked?
  • ☐ Are there at most two accent colours plus navy?
  • ☐ Is the word "learn" absent?
  • ☐ Is the CTA a yellow pill with navy text?
  • ☐ Is the rooster or the URL somewhere in the frame?
  • ☐ Does all type sit inside the platform safe area?
  • ☐ No gradients, no blurred shadows, no rounded-square-with-a-soft-shadow?
