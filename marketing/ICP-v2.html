Multi Lingo AI — Ideal Customer Profile (v2)

Practice any language for real life — slang, expressions and grammar you’ll actually use, alongside your teacher, class or app. Built by a working tutor.

Grasshopper IT Solutions LLC · v2 · 9 Sep 2026 · Purpose: brief for Meta Ads (later Google Ads) and the public landing page.

1. The one-paragraph ICP

An adult (28–48) who is already actively practising Portuguese, Spanish or English — with a tutor, a class, or 6+ months into a paid app — and who stalls between sessions. They don’t want another course; they want to keep the language warm, pick up how people actually speak, and walk into the next lesson or their next trip further ahead than they left it. They already pay for learning, so a few euro for the practice layer is an easy yes. They are reachable on Meta because they self-identify with language-learning, travel, expat and heritage-culture interests, and they convert on a free-to-start offer tied to a concrete moment (a trip, a plateau, a move).

3. How the ICP feels — before and with the app

Before (the itch)

  • Guilty — the streak is slipping and the lesson was days ago.
  • Stuck — “I’ve done months of this and still freeze in a real conversation.”
  • Self-conscious — sounds like a textbook; gets blank looks or a switch to English.
  • Frustrated — the app only teaches one dialect and assumes I think in English.
  • Impatient — wants the useful bits now, not lesson 1 again.

With Multi Lingo AI (the relief)

  • In control — practises the exact loose end (a word, a shaky rule), any order, any length.
  • Unblocked — grammar answered on demand, from real teaching, in their own language.
  • Capable — collects slang and local expressions that actually land.
  • Low-guilt — no streak to protect; five minutes counts.
  • Ahead — shows up to the lesson / the trip having gained ground.

4. Goals (self-achievement framing)

These are the outcomes the ICP is reaching for. Use this language in ad copy and on the landing page.

  • “Grow my vocabulary with words I’ll actually use” — everyday words, collocations, expressions — not word lists.
  • “Sound local, not foreign” — the right slang and register for my dialect (European vs Brazilian Portuguese, Spain vs Mexico).
  • “Get fluent with expressions” — stop translating in my head; reach for the phrase people really say.
  • “Fix the grammar that keeps tripping me up” — own the two or three rules I always get wrong.
  • “Be understood on my next trip / with my partner’s family” — a concrete, near-term social win.
  • “Make my lessons count for more” — arrive prepared, spend paid time on speaking, not review.
  • “Keep momentum without it running my life” — steady progress, on my schedule.

6. Reaching them & getting the registration (Meta Ads → landing page)

The landing page is the only real public surface. Every ad’s job is a click to the landing page and a free registration. Keep the funnel one step.

Audiences to build (Meta)

Cold — interestStack language-learning + adjacent intent: Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu, Rosetta Stone, iTalki, Preply, “language exchange”, Memrise, Pimsleur; plus context: Portugal / Spain / Mexico / Brazil travel, expat life, Erasmus, digital nomad, fado/flamenco, heritage-culture pages.
Cold — broadAdvantage+ / broad targeting with strong creative once the pixel has ~50 registrations/wk. Let Meta find them.
Geo overlaysHeritage clusters (NJ/MA/CA/RI, France, Luxembourg, Switzerland, UK) for the Reconnector; Lisbon/Porto/Madrid/Barcelona/CDMX for relocators & students.
Lookalikes1–3% LAL of: all registrants, then activated users (completed ≥1 practice session), then payers once volume allows. Payer LAL is the goal.
RetargetingLanding-page visitors who didn’t register (7/14/30d); registered-but-inactive; free users who hit the daily AI cap (upgrade ads).

Funnel & events

  • Objective: Sales/Leads → optimise for CompleteRegistration. Not link clicks.
  • Pixel/CAPI events: ViewContent (landing), CompleteRegistration (account created), StartTrial-equivalent (first practice session), Subscribe + value (paid).
  • Primary KPI: cost per registration. Secondary: % of registrations that complete a first session within 24h (creative-quality signal). North star: cost per activated user, then blended CAC vs. LTV.
  • Landing page must: match the ad’s promise above the fold, one CTA (“Start free”), social-proof line (“built by a working tutor”), no pricing wall before signup, 3-bullet “what you get”, FAQ answering “do I need a teacher?” and “is this a course?”.

Creative angles (test in parallel, 3–5 per persona)

  • Companion, not course — “Keep your tutor. Keep your app. Fill the gap between them.”
  • Sound local — textbook phrase vs. what people actually say (side-by-side).
  • Between lessons — the Tuesday-to-Tuesday memory drop-off.
  • Post-streak — “600 days in, still can’t hold a conversation.”
  • Heritage — “You understand your family. Now answer them.”
  • Your language, not English — the universal-input hook.
  • Trip countdown — “3 weeks to Lisbon. Practise what you’ll actually say.”
  • Format mix: founder-to-camera (tutor credibility), fast-cut UGC-style Reel, static carousel (feature tour), single-image (offer).

Words to use / avoid

Use

  • practice, keep it warm, sound local, real expressions, slang, alongside your teacher, in your language, 5 minutes, free to start

Avoid

  • learn / learning, course, curriculum, lessons (as our offer), fluent-in-3-months, streak, “master a language”, generic “AI-powered”

7. Not the ICP (exclude / suppress)

  • Absolute beginners with no teacher or course — there’s no syllabus to start them on.
  • “One app to replace everything” seekers — the pitch is deliberately a companion.
  • People who want live human conversation now — live tutor sessions are planned, not shipped.
  • Enterprise / L&D / schools — no team product today.
  • Bargain-only users who won’t pay and won’t return — fine on Free, don’t spend to acquire.
  • Learners of unsupported languages expecting Portuguese-level depth.

8. First 30 days — how to use this doc

  1. Ship a landing page variant per persona (same offer, different hero).
  2. Launch 1 campaign, 3 ad sets (interest / heritage-geo / broad), 3–5 creatives each, optimised for CompleteRegistration.
  3. Budget to ~50 registrations/week before judging; then build activated-user + payer lookalikes.
  4. Kill creatives below breakeven cost-per-registration; scale winners; add PT/ES ad sets.
  5. Revisit this doc monthly with real data: which persona actually registers cheapest, activates best, pays most.
